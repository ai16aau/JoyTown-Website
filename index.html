What the company does.

JoyTown Productions develops its own material and produces it end to end: script, financing, casting, crew, schedule, post and delivery.

It is the production banner of writer, producer and actor Ayo Hana, and it works at a scale that can actually be finished. Overthinking It was shot over two days in a single London flat, privately financed, with a paid cast and crew, and is in post-production for a 2027 festival run. The Budget Generation, five episodes of scripted audio, was written, produced and narrated in-house.

The standard does not change with the budget. Nothing is released unfinished, and everyone who works on a JoyTown production is contracted, paid and credited.
